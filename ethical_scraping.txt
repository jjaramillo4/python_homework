Why websites use robots.txt

robots.txt lets a site tell crawlers which areas they may and may not access.
It protects server resources and keeps non-content or administrative pages out
of automated crawls. Honoring it is the baseline of ethical scraping, because it
respects the site owner's stated wishes even though it is not legally enforced.

and the rules for specific user agents, is in assignment8/ethical_scraping.txt.
